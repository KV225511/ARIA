Council Verdict

Where the council agrees

  • Do not authorize the 600-episode run today. All five advisors require targeted fixes and a bounded canary.
  • Use stage-specific authorization. Generation, corpus admission, training, evaluation, and release need independent gates.
  • Freeze the contract before heavy spend. Pin model/config/code digests, schemas, split policy, action semantics, evaluator identity, hashes, and promotion rules.
  • Make the canary deterministic and measurable. It must stay quarantined and have declared stop, rollback, and promotion thresholds.

Where the council clashes

The Red Team treats mask handling, replay, evaluator integrity, OPE, and baselines as generation blockers. The majority defers genuinely downstream work unless it participates in generation or admission. The debate resolves this operationally: production-mask replay, independent evaluator disagreement, illegal-action mass, replay semantics, PII controls, immutable identity, and unbypassable quarantine are admission invariants. WIS/DR/FQE, baselines, fresh rollouts, mask-aware IQL, and release validation remain mandatory before learned-policy claims, but do not all need to block quarantined evidence generation.

Blind spots

The project lacks measured run economics, canary power, artifact-custody rules, thresholds, rollback/deletion rehearsal, and named owners with stop authority. Exact split counts may be tolerances rather than invariants; residency is primarily operational unless it changes model identity or determinism.

Recommendation

Block the 600-episode run for targeted corpus-integrity fixes and a deterministic synthetic-free adversarial canary; do not redesign yet. Approve full generation only after digest-pinned provenance, PII controls, fail-closed replay/audit checks, production-mask replay, independent evaluator validation, declared split tolerances, and unbypassable promotion rules pass. Keep training and evaluation separately blocked until mask-aware learning, locked evaluation, OPE, baselines, rollouts, and release-manifest gates pass.

One thing to do first

Write and freeze the machine-checkable invariant-to-stage contract assigning every gate a metric, threshold, evidence artifact, owner, stop authority, and promotion decision.

Dissent Ledger
  • A deterministic schema-valid canary can reproduce corrupted behavior when suspect mask, evaluator, replay, or split contracts participate; reproducibility alone is not validity.
  • Repair and bind the authoritative locked fixture before canary admission, with adversaries for premature termination, reordered turns, and illegal-action mass.
  • Require zero invariant violations, replay agreement, independent evaluator thresholds, lineage, and rollback/deletion rehearsal; quarantine must be technical.
  • Run the raw evidence gate before calibration, and keep omitted audit warnings, collapsed thresholds, unused rewards, residency uncertainty, and PII visible as stage-level risks.
